Prototype v0 · Hebrew verbs and words

Language Tool

Build and change Hebrew verb forms across binyanim, tenses and persons, and learn new words from context. What you get right quickly comes up less; what slows you down comes back.

Or tap "Comment" on any question, or any part of the Guide.
Forms are generated by rule from regular roots and checked against standard spelling without vowels. Some forms look identical (כתבת is "you wrote" for both a man and a woman); any matching spelling counts. Answers that take over 30 seconds are treated as a pause and left out of your times. Progress is kept in this browser only; copy it from the Progress tab to save it.
